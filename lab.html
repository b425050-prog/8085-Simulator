THE LAB WORKFLOW

One board. One line at a time.

  1. Connect AC power and the PS/2 keyboard. Switch the trainer ON.
  2. Click the green LCD or “Type on board”. Your keyboard controls the trainer.
  3. Press 1, then A. Type 2000 and Enter.
  4. Type an instruction, such as MVI A 80, and Enter. A valid line is stored and the next address appears. A syntax error keeps the same address. Use exactly one space; commas only in MOV A,B, no H suffix. Backspace/Delete are inactive; Reset, return to the address and retype to correct a mistake.
  5. Press Reset. Press G, type 2000, then . to execute.
  6. Press R then A to inspect A, or M then an address to inspect memory.

Click connector housings for full ribbon or supply cables. Individual pins accept jumpers. Scope probes need both a signal tip and a ground clip. Scroll or drag knobs.
